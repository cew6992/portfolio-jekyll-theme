Lost Pet Recovery

Helping pet owners know what to do next when their pet goes missing.

The Problem

When a pet goes missing, owners suddenly have dozens of things they could do: search the neighborhood, contact shelters, create flyers, post online, contact their microchip company, and notify people nearby.

The information exists, but figuring out what to do first can be overwhelming.

The Product Question

How might AI help someone quickly understand what to do next based on their pet, location, and circumstances?

The Concept

Lost Pet Recovery is an AI-assisted experience that turns information about a missing pet into a personalized recovery plan.

Rather than giving someone another generic checklist, the goal is to prioritize the actions most relevant to the situation.

What I Built

Initial product strategy and MVP definition
Personalized recovery-plan concept
Printable 8.5 × 11 lost-pet flyer
High-visibility 11 × 17 lost-pet poster
Early thinking around AI-driven next-step recommendations

Product Principles

Reduce decisions. The experience should tell someone what matters now rather than presenting another long list.

Make outputs useful immediately. Generated materials should be ready to print, post, or share.

Keep the owner in control. AI can organize and prioritize information, but the experience should remain straightforward and transparent.

What I Learned

The value of AI isn't generating more lost-pet information. There is already plenty of information available.

The more interesting opportunity is using AI to turn scattered guidance into prioritized, situational actions.

What's Next

The next step is to prototype the guided recovery experience and determine the minimum information needed to generate a genuinely useful action plan.
